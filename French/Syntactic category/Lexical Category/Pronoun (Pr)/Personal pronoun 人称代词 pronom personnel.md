# Introduction 

Personal pronouns (*pronoms personnels*) designate the participants of speech by [[Person 人称 personne|person]] (1st: speaker, 2nd: addressee, 3rd: other) and replace noun phrases. They vary in person, number, gender (3rd person) and **function** (subject, COD, COI, …). See also [[Pronoun 代词 pronom|pronoun]].

# Classification 

| person       | subject (clitic) | COD     | COI      | reflexive | tonic        |
| ------------ | ---------------- | ------- | -------- | --------- | ------------ |
| 1st pers. s. | je (j')          | me (m') | me (m')  | me (m')   | moi          |
| 2nd pers s.  | tu               | te (t') | te (t')  | te (t')   | toi          |
| 3rd pers. s. | il / elle / on   | le / la (l') | lui | se (s')   | lui / elle / soi |
| 1st pers. p. | nous             | nous    | nous     | nous      | nous         |
| 2nd pers p.  | vous             | vous    | vous     | vous      | vous         |
| 3rd pers. p. | ils / elles      | les     | leur     | se (s')   | eux / elles  |

Plus the adverbial pronouns **y** (*à* + thing / place) and **en** (*de* + noun, partitive, quantity).

- **Clitic** (atone, 非重读) pronouns are attached to the verb and cannot stand alone.
- **Tonic** (重读) pronouns are autonomous: they can be stressed, stand alone, follow a preposition.

## Clitic pronouns

### Personal clitic pronoun as subject

#### Forms

*je, tu, il, elle, on, nous, vous, ils, elles*. *je* elides before a vowel: *j'aime* (see [[Elision 省音 élision|elision]]). Liaison with *nous, vous, ils, elles* before a vowel: *nous‿avons, ils‿ont* (see [[Liaison 联诵 liaison|liaison]]).

#### Use 

- *tu* (familiar, singular) vs *vous* (polite singular or any plural).
> **Vous** êtes prêt, Monsieur ? / **Vous** êtes prêts, les enfants ?
- *il / elle* refer to persons and things according to grammatical gender: *la table → **elle**.*
- *on*: indefinite (*people, one*) or, in speech, = *nous*. The verb is always 3rd person singular; the attribute may agree with the meaning: *On est content**s**.*
- *il* impersonal: *il pleut, il faut, il y a* (see [[Subject 主语 sujet|subject]]).
- *ils* for a mixed group (masculine prevails).

#### Position

Before the verb (and before object clitics and *ne*). After the verb in [[Inversion 倒装 inversion|inversion]], joined by a hyphen, with *-t-* between two vowels:

> **Parlez-vous** français ? **A-t-il** fini ?

### Object clitic pronouns

They go **immediately before the verb** they depend on (before the auxiliary in compound tenses, before the infinitive in verb + infinitive):

> Je **la** vois. Je **l'**ai vue. Je vais **la** voir. Je **ne la** vois **pas**.

The choice between COD (*le/la/les*), COI (*lui/leur*), *y* and *en* depends on the verb's construction; see [[Object 宾语 objet|object]].

> Je connais **Paul**. → Je **le** connais.
> Je téléphone **à Paul**. → Je **lui** téléphone.
> Je vais **à Paris**. → J'**y** vais.
> Je veux **du café**. → J'**en** veux.

*le* can also be neuter, replacing an adjective or a clause: *Tu es fatiguée ? — Oui, je **le** suis.*

### Summary of order for clitic object pronouns

Declarative sentences and negative imperative:

| 1               | 2              | 3            | 4   | 5   |
| --------------- | -------------- | ------------ | --- | --- |
| me, te, se, nous, vous | le, la, les | lui, leur | y   | en  |

> Il **me le** donne. Je **le lui** donne. Il **y en** a. Ne **me le** dis pas.

*me/te/se/nous/vous* and *lui/leur* cannot co-occur: *Je **te** présente **à lui**.*

Affirmative imperative: pronouns follow the verb, hyphenated; COD before COI; *me/te* become *moi/toi* (except before *en*):

| verb | le, la, les | moi, toi, lui, nous, vous, leur | y | en |
| ---- | ----------- | ------------------------------- | - | -- |

> Donne-**le-moi**. Donnez-**lui-en**. Va-**t'en**.

## Tonic pronouns 

*moi, toi, lui, elle, soi, nous, vous, eux, elles*

### Use 

1. Emphasis, contrast:
> **Moi**, je reste. **Lui**, il part.
2. Alone, without a verb:
> Qui veut du café ? — **Moi** !
3. After a preposition:
> Je pense **à toi**. Il est parti **sans eux**. C'est **à moi**. (possession)
4. After *c'est / ce sont*:
> C'est **lui**. Ce sont **eux**.
5. In comparisons, after *que*:
> Il est plus grand **que moi**.
6. Coordinated subjects:
> **Toi et moi**, nous partirons.
7. With *-même*, *seul*, *aussi*:
> Je l'ai fait **moi-même**. **Lui seul** le sait. **Moi aussi**.
8. *soi*: with indefinite subjects (*on, chacun, personne*):
> **Chacun** pour **soi**.

### Position 

Free: detached at the beginning or end of the sentence (*Moi, je… / Je ne sais pas, moi.*), after a preposition, or after the imperative (*Regarde-**moi**.*). It never replaces the clitic subject, it doubles it: *✗ Moi pense* → ***Moi**, **je** pense.*

# Comparison with English

- English has one object form (*him, her, them*); French distinguishes COD / COI and adds *y, en*.
- French object pronouns precede the verb (except in the affirmative imperative): *I see **her*** → *Je **la** vois.*
- English stresses a pronoun by intonation; French uses a tonic pronoun or *c'est … qui*.
