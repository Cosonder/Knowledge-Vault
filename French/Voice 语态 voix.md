# Definition

Voice（语态, *la voix*）describes the relation between the action expressed by the verb and its participants: whether the [[Subject 主语 sujet|subject]] performs the action (agent) or undergoes it (patient)。语态是动词的一个语法范畴，通过助动词和动词形式的变化来体现。

# Classification

French grammar traditionally distinguishes three voices:
1. **voix active** 主动语态：the subject is the agent.
2. **voix passive** 被动语态：the subject is the patient; the agent is optional, introduced by *par* / *de*.
3. **voix pronominale** 代词式语态：the verb is accompanied by a reflexive pronoun (*se*) agreeing with the subject. See [[Pronominal verb 代动词 verbe pronominal|pronominal verb]].

# Active voice

The default voice. The subject performs the action, a direct object（COD, see [[Object 宾语 objet|object]]）may follow.

> Le chat **mange** la souris.
> The cat eats the mouse.

# Passive voice

## Formation

passive = **être** (conjugated in the required tense and mood) + [[Past participle 过去分词 participe passé|past participle]]

The tense is carried by *être*; the past participle always agrees in gender and number with the subject (see [[Agreement 配合 accord|agreement]]).

> La souris **est mangée** par le chat.
> The mouse is eaten by the cat.

| Tense (active)       | Active                | Passive                      |
| -------------------- | --------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| présent              | il construit la maison | la maison **est construite** |
| passé composé        | il a construit         | elle **a été construite**    |
| imparfait            | il construisait        | elle **était construite**    |
| futur simple         | il construira          | elle **sera construite**     |
| subjonctif présent   | qu'il construise       | qu'elle **soit construite**  |

Note: in compound passive tenses, *été* never agrees (*elle a **été** construite*), only the final participle agrees.

## Conditions

Only transitive verbs with a COD can be passivized. The COD of the active sentence becomes the subject of the passive.

> On a donné un livre **à Marie**.
> ❌ *Marie a été donnée un livre.*

与英语不同：英语可以把间接宾语变成被动句主语（*Mary was given a book*），法语不行，只能说 *On a donné un livre à Marie.* Exceptions: *obéir, désobéir, pardonner* (historically transitive) can be passive: *Il sera obéi.*

## Agent: *par* vs *de*

- **par**: concrete action, the general case.
> Le voleur a été arrêté **par** la police.
- **de**: states, feelings, description (*aimer, respecter, connaître, entourer, couvrir*...).
> Il est aimé **de** tous.
> La table est couverte **de** livres.

# Alternatives to the passive

French uses the passive less often than English. Common alternatives:

1. Indefinite subject *on* (see [[Indefinite words 泛指词 mots indéfinis|indefinite words]]):
> **On** parle français ici.
> French is spoken here.
2. Passive pronominal (*se* + verb), for general truths, with an inanimate subject:
> Ce livre **se vend** bien.
> This book sells well.
> Le vin blanc **se boit** frais.
3. *se faire* / *se laisser* + infinitive, when the subject is a person:
> Il **s'est fait** voler son portefeuille.
> He had his wallet stolen.

# Remarks

1. Distinguish passive *être* + past participle from compound tenses of *être*-verbs: *Elle **est** partie* is the active passé composé of *partir*, not a passive. Test: a passive has an active counterpart with a COD.
2. A passive sentence often expresses a resulting state: *La porte est fermée* = the door is (in the state of being) closed. Here the participle behaves like an adjective.
