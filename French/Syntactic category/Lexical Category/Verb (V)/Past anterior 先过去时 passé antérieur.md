# Introduction
The past anterior is the compound form of the [[Past historic 简单过去时 passé simple|past historic]]

Like the passé simple, it belongs to **literary written narration**. It expresses an action completed **immediately before** another past action in the passé simple. 口语中不用。

# Formation

the past anterior = the passé simple of *être/avoir* + the [[Past participle 过去分词 Participe passé|past participle]]

Auxiliary choice and [[Agreement 配合 accord|agreement]] follow the [[Perfect 复合过去时 Passé composé|passé composé]].

|              | finir (avoir)    | partir (être)              |
| ------------ | ---------------- | -------------------------- |
| 1st pers. s. | j'eus fini       | je fus parti(e)            |
| 2nd pers s.  | tu eus fini      | tu fus parti(e)            |
| 3rd pers. s. | il eut fini      | il fut parti / elle fut partie |
| 1st pers. p. | nous eûmes fini  | nous fûmes parti(e)s       |
| 2nd pers p.  | vous eûtes fini  | vous fûtes parti(e)(s)     |
| 3rd pers. p. | ils eurent fini  | ils furent partis / elles furent parties |

Others: *il eut été, il eut eu, il eut fait, il fut allé, il se fut levé*.

注意不要与虚拟式愈过去时混淆：*qu'il **eût** fini*（有长音符）是 subjonctif plus-que-parfait，*il **eut** fini* 才是 passé antérieur。

# Use

## In temporal subordinate clauses

Mainly after *quand, lorsque, dès que, aussitôt que, après que, à peine… que*, with the main clause in the passé simple.

> **Dès qu'**il **eut fini** son repas, il sortit.
> -> As soon as he had finished his meal, he went out.

> **À peine** **fut**-elle **arrivée** **qu'**on l'appela.
> -> Hardly had she arrived when she was called.

## In a main clause

Rarer: with an adverb such as *bientôt, vite, en un instant*, it stresses rapid completion.

> En un instant, il **eut compris**.
> -> In an instant he had understood.

## Past anterior vs pluperfect

| passé antérieur                               | plus-que-parfait                                   |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| paired with a **passé simple**                 | paired with imparfait, passé composé or passé simple |
| single action, **immediately** before          | anteriority in general, possibly distant           |
| literary only                                 | all registers                                      |
| often in subordinate clauses of time           | main and subordinate clauses                        |

> Quand il **eut terminé**, il partit. (once, right after)
> Quand il **avait terminé**, il partait. (habit: every time)

In modern written French the [[Pluperfect 愈过去时 plus-que-parfait|pluperfect]] increasingly replaces the passé antérieur, and in speech the [[Double compound past 超复合过去时 passé surcomposé|passé surcomposé]] can play its role alongside the passé composé.

# English equivalent

*had* + past participle (*had finished*), often after *as soon as, when, hardly … when*.
