# Category

There are two main verb categories:
- constructed:
	- reflexive 
	- reciprocal
- idiomatic (lexicalized)
## Reflexive and reciprocal

In this category, when ***se*** is added to a verb, the meaning remains the same in the pronominal form.
> *regarder* (to look at)
> *se regarder* is the corresponding pronominal verb.
> -> reflexive: to look at oneself;
> -> reciprocal: to look at one another.

Rmk. Some pronominal verbs are either only reflexive or only reciprocal.

### Reflexive verbs 反身意义 sens réfléchi
The subject performs the action on him/herself, directly or indirectly.
> *Je **me** réveille tous les matins à sept heures*
> (*réveiller qn*: *me* refers directly to *je*)
> -> I wake up every morning at 7 o'clock.
> 
> *Il ne se rase jamais le dimanche.*


### Reciprocal verbs 相互意义 sens réciproque

## Idiomatic or inherent (lexicalized) pronominal verbs

There are two categories of idiomatic pronominal verbs.

### 带 *se* 后意义发生变化

Those which by adding ***se*** to the verb, acquire a meaning which is linked in some way to that of the original verb.


| Original verb | Meaning           | Pronominal verb    | Meaning                   |
| ------------- | ----------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------- |
| *attendre*    | to wait           | *s'attendre à*     | to expect                 |
| *douter*      | to doubt          | *se douter de*      | to suspect                |
| *rappeler*    | to remind         | *se rappeler*      | to remember               |
| *servir*      | to serve          | *se servir \[de\]* | to use; to help oneself   |
| *demander*    | to ask            | *se demander*      | to wonder, to ask oneself |
| *taire*       | to keep sth quiet | *se taire*         | to be/remain silent       |
### 本质代动词 verbes essentiellement pronominaux

Those which only exist in pronominal form.

## Agreement


### In person
In the conjugation of a pronominal verb, the pronoun *se* changes according to the person of the **subject**.

| person subject | reflexive pronoun |
| -------------- | ----------------- |
| je             | me(m')            |
| tu             | te(t')            |
| il/elle        | se(s')            |
| nous           | nous              |
| vous           | vous              |
| ils/elles      | se(s')            |

#### Infinitive and present participle 
***se*** changes with the subject, even if the verb is in the infinitive or is a present participle.

> ***Nous** allons **nous promener**.*
> -> We are going for a walk.
> *Je **leur** ai dit de **se taire**.*
> -> I told them to stop talking.
#### Compound tenses
In compound tenses, all pronominal verbs are conjugated with ***être***.
这是最重要的形式规则之一:  **所有代动词的复合时态都使用 être。**
#### Past participle 
##### Reflexive and reciprocal
Whether the pronominal verb is reflexive or reciprocal, *se* can be a direct or an indirect object (see 3.1.1 above). This is important for past participle agreement.

###### *se* as COD : agreement
The past participle agrees in gender and number with the subject in the following cases (‘*se*’ is the direct object).
> - reflexive:
> 	***Elle s'** est coupé**e**.*
> 	She cut herself.
> - reciprocal:
> 	***Ils se** sont aimé**s** pendant trois ans.*
> 	They loved each other for three years.

判断方法非常简单：看原动词是不是： verbe + quelqu’un
###### *se* as COI : no agreement
如果原动词结构是：

> verbe **à quelqu’un**

那么 se 就是 COI。
> 
##### Lexicalized 

*se* is part of the verb and cannot be analysed separately. In compound tenses, the past participle agrees with the subject of the verb.

> *Elle s’est rendue à Paris en septembre.*
> She went to Paris in September.

Note the following exceptions, depending on the meaning of the verb:

– *s’imaginer que* to imagine that

– *se plaire (à faire qch)* to enjoy (doing sth)

– *se rendre compte (de qch)* to realize (sth)

– *se rire (de qn)* to mock (sb)

With these four verbs, the past participle remains invariable.
## Position of *se*

The following cases should be examined for both simple and compound tenses and where relevant, for present participles and infinitives. Note that the pronoun *se* is always immediately before the verb (or before the auxiliary in compound tenses), except in the case of the affirmative imperative, when it is placed immediately after the verb and hyphenated. This rule only applies if there is no other personal pronoun. See also chapters 14 Imperative and 31 Personal Pronouns.

### With other pronouns
代词的基本顺序仍然是：

> **me / te / se / nous / vous → le / la / les → lui / leur → y → en**

例如：

> Je **me le** demande.  
> 我也在问自己这个问题 / 我想知道。

> Il **s’en** souvient.  
> 他记得这件事。

> Elle **s’y** intéresse.  
> 她对此感兴趣。

> Elle **ne s’en est pas rendu compte**.  
> 她没有意识到这一点。

所以会大量见到：

> **s’en**  
> **s’y**

例如：

> s’en aller  
> s’en souvenir  
> s’en sortir  
> s’y intéresser  
> s’y habituer
