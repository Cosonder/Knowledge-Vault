# Definition

Person（人称, *la personne*）is the grammatical category that distinguishes the participants of the speech act:
- **1st person**: the speaker (*je, nous*);
- **2nd person**: the addressee (*tu, vous*);
- **3rd person**: neither speaker nor addressee, what is talked about (*il, elle, on, ils, elles*).

In French, person is marked on [[Personal pronoun 人称代词 pronom personnel|personal pronouns]], possessive determiners/pronouns and on finite verbs. Together with number it forms the person × number grid used for [[Conjugation 变位 conjugaison|conjugation]]. 限定动词与主语在人称和数上配合，见 [[Agreement 配合 accord|agreement]]。

# Paradigm

|              | subject pronoun | example (*parler*) | example (*être*) |
| ------------ | --------------- | ------------------ | ---------------- |
| 1st pers. s. | je              | je parle           | je suis          |
| 2nd pers s.  | tu              | tu parles          | tu es            |
| 3rd pers. s. | il / elle / on  | il parle           | il est           |
| 1st pers. p. | nous            | nous parlons       | nous sommes      |
| 2nd pers p.  | vous            | vous parlez        | vous êtes        |
| 3rd pers. p. | ils / elles     | ils parlent        | ils sont         |

# Remarks

## *tu* vs *vous*

*vous* is both the 2nd person plural and the polite singular (*vouvoiement*). The verb is always plural, but adjectives and participles agree with the real referent:
> **Vous êtes** contente, madame ?
> Are you happy, madam? (one woman → *contente*, singular feminine)

英语只有 *you*，不区分亲疏；法语用 *tu*（tutoiement）对熟人、家人、孩子，用 *vous* 对陌生人、长辈、正式场合。

## *on*

Formally 3rd person singular, but its meaning can be:
- indefinite "one / people": **On** dit que... (they say that)
- 1st person plural in spoken French, replacing *nous*:
> **On** est arrivés hier.
> We arrived yesterday.

The verb stays singular; attributes and participles may agree with the meaning (*arrivés*).

## Impersonal 3rd person

Some verbs are used only with the impersonal *il*, which refers to nothing (无人称):
> **Il** pleut. **Il** faut partir. **Il** y a un problème.

## Person hierarchy in coordination

1st > 2nd > 3rd (see [[Agreement 配合 accord|agreement]]):
> Toi et moi, **nous** sommes amis.
> Paul et toi, **vous** êtes amis.

## Imperative

The imperative only has three persons: 2nd s., 1st p., 2nd p. (*parle, parlons, parlez*). See [[Mood 语气 mode|mood]].
