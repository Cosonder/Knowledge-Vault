# Definition

Aspect（体, *l'aspect*）describes how the speaker views the internal temporal structure of a process: as completed or ongoing, as a whole or in progress, as beginning, repeated, etc. 与 [[Tense 时态 temps|tense]] 不同：tense 把事件定位在时间轴上（过去/现在/将来），aspect 关注事件本身的“内部构造”。

法语没有像俄语那样的系统性形态体，但体的对立融入了时态系统（尤其是过去时态），并通过迂回结构（périphrases）表达。

# Classification

## Grammatical aspect (aspect grammatical)

Expressed by verb forms.

### Perfective vs imperfective (accompli / non accompli; perfectif / imperfectif)

| Perfective (process seen as a whole, bounded)       | Imperfective (process seen from inside, unbounded) |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| [[Perfect 复合过去时 Passé composé\|passé composé]]   | [[Imperfect 未完成过去时 Imparfait\|imparfait]]       |
| [[Past historic 简单过去时 passé simple\|passé simple]] |                                                    |

> Hier, il **a plu** toute la journée.
> It rained all day (event viewed as a closed whole).

> Quand je suis sorti, il **pleuvait**.
> It was raining (background, ongoing).

This is the central difficulty for English speakers: English *was raining* ≈ imparfait, but English simple past *rained* can correspond to either imparfait (habit, state) or passé composé (event).

> Quand j'étais petit, je **jouais** au foot.
> When I was little, I played football (habit → imparfait).

### Simple vs compound forms (forme simple / forme composée)

Every simple tense has a compound counterpart (auxiliary + past participle) expressing **accompli**: the process is completed relative to the reference point. See the table in [[Tense 时态 temps|tense]].

> Quand il **arrive**, je **pars**. (simultaneous)
> Quand il **arrive**, je **suis** déjà **parti**. (completed before)

## Lexical aspect (aspect lexical, *Aktionsart*)

Inherent to the verb meaning:
- **perfectif / borné**: the process has a natural end (*sortir, naître, trouver*).
- **imperfectif / non borné**: no inherent end (*marcher, aimer, vivre*).

## Periphrastic aspect (périphrases aspectuelles)

| Aspect                   | Construction                                    | Example                                  |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| progressive 进行          | *être en train de* + inf.                       | Je **suis en train de** lire.            |
| prospective 即将          | *aller* + inf. / *être sur le point de* + inf.  | Il **va** pleuvoir.                      |
| recent past 刚刚          | *venir de* + inf.                               | Elle **vient de** partir.                |
| inchoative 开始           | *commencer à*, *se mettre à* + inf.             | Il **s'est mis à** pleurer.              |
| continuative 继续         | *continuer à / de* + inf.                       | Elle **continue de** travailler.         |
| terminative 结束          | *finir de*, *cesser de* + inf.                  | J'**ai fini de** manger.                 |
| iterative 反复            | prefix *re-*                                    | Il **relit** la lettre.                  |

# Remarks

1. French present tense covers both English *I read* and *I am reading*: *Je lis.* The progressive *être en train de* is used only to insist on the ongoing nature.
2. *venir de* + inf. is only used in the présent and imparfait: *Il venait de partir* = he had just left.
