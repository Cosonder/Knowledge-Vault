# Definition

An exclamative structure（感叹结构, *la phrase exclamative*）expresses a strong feeling of the speaker (surprise, admiration, indignation...) about a degree or a quantity. In writing it ends with **!** (preceded by a non-breaking space in French typography). 与疑问句不同，感叹句一般**不倒装**主语。

# Classification

## *Quel* + noun

*quel* is a determiner and agrees in gender and number with the noun (see [[Agreement 配合 accord|agreement]]):

|    | m.     | f.       |
| -- | ------ | -------- |
| s. | quel   | quelle   |
| p. | quels  | quelles  |

> **Quel** beau temps !
> What lovely weather!
> **Quelle** surprise !
> **Quels** idiots !

No article after *quel* (英语 *What a surprise!* 有 *a*，法语没有：❌ *Quelle une surprise*).

With a verb: no inversion.
> **Quelle** erreur j'ai faite !

Note the agreement of the past participle with a preceding COD (see [[Perfect 复合过去时 Passé composé|passé composé]]): *faite* agrees with *quelle erreur*.

## *Que* / *Comme* / *Ce que* / *Qu'est-ce que* + clause

Exclamation about degree ("how ...!"), the clause keeps normal word order.
> **Que** c'est beau !
> **Comme** il est gentil !
> **Ce que** tu es bête ! (familiar)
> **Qu'est-ce qu'**il fait chaud ! (familiar)

与英语对比：*How beautiful it is!* 中形容词紧跟 *how*；法语 *comme/que* 后形容词留在原位（❌ *Comme gentil il est !*）。

## Quantity: *que de* / *combien de* + noun

> **Que de** monde !
> So many people!
> **Combien de** fois je te l'ai dit !

## Intensifiers

*tellement, si, tant*, often with suspension:
> Il est **tellement** gentil !
> J'ai **si** faim !
> Il a **tant** travaillé !

## Interjections and nominal exclamations

> **Ah !** **Oh là là !** **Zut !** **Bravo !**
> **Silence !** **Au secours !**

## Exclamative infinitive and *si* clauses

> Moi, **mentir** ! (indignation)
> **Si** j'avais su ! (regret: if only I had known)

# Remarks

1. *Quel* exclamative and *quel* interrogative have the same forms; intonation and punctuation distinguish them: *Quel livre ?* / *Quel livre !*
2. *Que* exclamative (degree) should not be confused with interrogative *que* (*Que fais-tu ?*, inversion) or the subjunctive *que* of wishes (*Qu'il entre !*).
3. In literary style, a negative rhetorical exclamation with inversion expresses a high degree: *Que **ne** ferait-il **pas** pour elle !* (What wouldn't he do for her!)
