# Introduction

The past historic (*le passé simple*) is the simple perfective past tense of the indicative. It is the **narrative tense of written French** (novels, history, tales, journalism of a literary kind). 口语中几乎不用，由 [[Perfect 复合过去时 Passé composé|passé composé]] 替代；但阅读必须能识别，尤其是第三人称形式（*il fut, il eut, il fit, il vint*）。

Its compound form is the [[Past anterior 先过去时 passé antérieur|past anterior]].

# Formation

The stem is usually the infinitive minus *-er/-ir/-re*, but many irregular verbs build it on the [[Past participle 过去分词 Participe passé|past participle]] (*eu → j'eus, lu → je lus, pris → je pris*). There are three vowel types: **-a-**, **-i-**, **-u-** (plus *-in-* for *venir, tenir*).

## Desinences

|              | -er verbs | -ir / -re (type i) | type u  |
| ------------ | --------- | ------------------ | ------- |
| 1st pers. s. | -ai       | -is                | -us     |
| 2nd pers s.  | -as       | -is                | -us     |
| 3rd pers. s. | -a        | -it                | -ut     |
| 1st pers. p. | -âmes     | -îmes              | -ûmes   |
| 2nd pers p.  | -âtes     | -îtes              | -ûtes   |
| 3rd pers. p. | -èrent    | -irent             | -urent  |

注意 *nous / vous* 的长音符（accent circonflexe），以及 -er 动词第三人称复数 **-èrent**。

## Regular verbs

|              | parler     | finir      | vendre     |
| ------------ | ---------- | ---------- | ---------- |
| 1st pers. s. | parlai     | finis      | vendis     |
| 2nd pers s.  | parlas     | finis      | vendis     |
| 3rd pers. s. | parla      | finit      | vendit     |
| 1st pers. p. | parlâmes   | finîmes    | vendîmes   |
| 2nd pers p.  | parlâtes   | finîtes    | vendîtes   |
| 3rd pers. p. | parlèrent  | finirent   | vendirent  |

*je finis, tu finis, il finit* are identical to the present; context decides.

Spelling: *je commençai, il mangea* (-cer/-ger before *a*).

|              | être     | avoir    | aller     | faire    | venir     |

| 1st pers. s. | fus      | eus      | allai     | fis      | vins      |
| 2nd pers s.  | fus      | eus      | allas     | fis      | vins      |
| 3rd pers. s. | fut      | eut      | alla      | fit      | vint      |

# Use

## Narration of completed events

Each event is seen as a whole, a point on the time line; successive passés simples move the story forward.

> Il **entra**, **posa** son chapeau et **s'assit**.
> -> He came in, put down his hat and sat down.

## Historical facts

> Napoléon **mourut** à Sainte-Hélène en 1821.

## Passé simple vs imparfait

The same division of labour as passé composé vs imparfait: the passé simple carries the events, the [[Imperfect 未完成过去时 Imparfait|imparfait]] the background.

> Il **pleuvait** quand elle **sortit**.
> -> It was raining when she went out.

## Passé simple vs passé composé

| passé simple                          | passé composé                              |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| written, literary register            | spoken and everyday written register       |
| cut off from the present (récit)      | linked to the present (discours) or neutral |
| rarely used in 1st/2nd person         | all persons                                |

> Victor Hugo **naquit** en 1802. (written narrative)
> Victor Hugo **est né** en 1802. (neutral)

# English equivalent

The simple past (*he came, he said*).
