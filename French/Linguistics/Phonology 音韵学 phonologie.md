# Definition

**Phonology**（音韵学，音位学）is the branch of linguistics that studies how sounds function as a system in a particular language: which sounds contrast ([[Phoneme 音位 phonème|phonemes]]), how they are realized ([[Phone 音素 phone|allophones]]), how they combine (phonotactics), and how they change in context (phonological processes).

与 [[Phonetics 语音学 phonétique|phonetics]] 的区别：语音学问“这个音是怎么发出来的”，音韵学问“这个音在该语言的系统里起什么作用”。

# Classification (main topics)

1. **Segmental phonology**: the phoneme inventory and its oppositions.
2. **Phonotactics**（音位配列）: which sequences are allowed. French permits /ps/ initially (*psychologie*), English does not pronounce it (*psychology*).
3. **Syllable structure**: French strongly prefers open syllables (CV) and resyllabifies across word boundaries.
4. **Suprasegmentals / prosody**（超音段）: stress, intonation, tone. French stress falls on the last syllable of the rhythmic group, not of each word.
5. **Phonological processes**: assimilation, deletion, insertion, resyllabification.

# Examples (French processes at word boundaries)

[[Liaison 联诵 liaison|Liaison]] — a latent final consonant surfaces before a vowel:
> le**s** amis /le.z‿a.mi/

[[Linking 连音 enchaînement|Enchaînement]] — a pronounced final consonant is resyllabified:
> e**ll**e arrive \[ɛ.la.ʁiv\]

[[Elision 省音 élision|Elision]] — a final vowel is dropped before a vowel:
> **l'**ami (not \*le ami)

Avoidance of [[Hiatus 元音连续 hiatus|hiatus]] — insertion of /t/:
> Va**-t-**il ?

Schwa deletion (*e muet*):
> sam(e)di \[samdi\], je n(e) sais pas \[ʒənsɛpa\]; colloquial j(e) sais pas \[ʃɛpa\]

Voicing assimilation:
> mé**d**ecin \[metsɛ̃\]（/d/ 在清辅音前清化）

# Remarks

1. 在中文传统中，“音韵学”专指汉语历史音韵（声韵调、反切、韵书）；现代语言学的 phonology 更常译作“音系学”或“音位学”。
2. Many apparent "spelling rules" of French are in fact phonological rules; [[Orthography 正字法 orthographe|orthography]] often preserves forms that phonology has changed (silent final consonants).
