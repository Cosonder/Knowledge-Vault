# Definition

A **word**（词，mot）is traditionally defined as the smallest unit of language that can stand on its own and carry meaning. It is built from one or more morphemes (see [[Morphology 构词学 morphologie|morphology]]) and is the basic unit of the [[Lexicon 词库 lexique|lexicon]] and of [[Lexicology 词汇学 lexicologie|lexicology]].

"Word" is intuitive but hard to define precisely. Linguists distinguish several senses:

1. **Orthographic word**（书写词）: a string between spaces. *pomme de terre* = 3 orthographic words.
2. **Phonological word**（音系词）: a unit of stress and sound processes. In French, *les amis* /le.z‿a.mi/ behaves as one rhythmic unit.
3. **Grammatical word / word-form**（词形）: one particular inflected form — *mange, mangeons*.
4. **Lexeme**（词位，lexème）: the abstract dictionary unit uniting all its forms — MANGER. See [[Inflection 屈折变化 flexion|inflection]].

# Classification

## By structure
* **Simple word**（单纯词，mot simple）: *table, chat*.
* **Derived word**（派生词，mot dérivé）: *chant**eur***, ***in**connu*.
* **Compound word**（复合词，mot composé）: *porte-clés*, *chemin de fer*.

## By function
* **Content / lexical words**（实词，mots lexicaux）: noun, verb, adjective, adverb — open classes, new ones are constantly created.
* **Function / grammatical words**（虚词，mots grammaticaux）: article, preposition, conjunction, pronoun — closed classes. *le, de, et, que, il*.

See the syntactic categories under `Syntactic category/Lexical Category`.

# Examples where "word" boundaries blur in French

Clitic pronouns attach to the verb and cannot stand alone:
> Je **le lui** donne.
> Donne-**le-moi** !

[[Elision 省音 élision|Elision]] fuses two words in writing:
> **l'**homme, **j'**ai, **qu'**il

Contracted articles are two morphemes in one form:
> **au** = à + le, **du** = de + le, **aux** = à + les

Compounds written with spaces are still one lexical unit:
> **pomme de terre** 土豆 (≠ "apple of earth")

# Contrast with Chinese

汉语书面语不用空格分词，“词”与“字”的区分尤其重要：“字”是书写单位（大致对应语素），“词”可以是单字（书）或多字（图书馆）。分词（word segmentation）因此是中文 NLP 的独立问题，而法语、英语靠空格就基本解决。
