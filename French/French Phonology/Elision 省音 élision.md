# Definition

少数单词（多为单音节单词）如果后面跟的是以元音或哑音h开头的词，会因为读音的关系省略掉词末的元音字母，改写为省文撇'（apostrophe），这种现象被称为省音（élision）。如：ce + est -> c'est, que + il -> qu'il

九个以 e 结尾的单音节词 ce, de, je, le, me, ne, que, se, te 都遵循这一规则。此外，la, si, jusque, lorsque, presque, puisque 等词也有省音现象。

Elision avoids a [[Hiatus 元音连续 hiatus|hiatus]] at a word boundary. It is both a phonological process (the vowel is not pronounced) and an orthographic one (it is written with an apostrophe); see [[Phonology 音韵学 phonologie|phonology]] and [[Orthography 正字法 orthographe|orthography]].

# Classification
* personal pronoun: *je, me, te, le, la, se*.
* demonstrative pronoun: *ce*.
* article: *le,la*.
* relative pronoun: *que*
* preposition: *de*.
* negation adverb: *ne*.
* conjunction: *que* (and its compounds *lorsque, puisque, quoique*), *si*.

Note: *que* can be a relative pronoun, a conjunction or an interrogative pronoun; *si* is a conjunction. Both elide.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **le, la** | article | **l'**ami, **l'**heure | — |
| **le, la** | object pronoun | je **l'**aime | not after an imperative: *Prends-**le** avec toi* |
| **je** | subject pronoun | **j'**ai | not after inversion: *Puis-**je** entrer ?* |
| **me, te, se** | pronouns | il **m'**aime, **s'**appeler | also *moi, toi* → *m', t'* before *en, y*: *Donne-**m'en***, *Va-**t'en*** |
| **ce** | demonstrative pronoun | **c'**est, **c'**était, **ç'**a été | only before *être* (and *en*: *c'en est trop*); not the determiner (*cet homme*) |
| **de** | preposition / article | **d'**abord, pas **d'**argent | — |
| **ne** | negation | je **n'**ai pas | — |
| **que** | relative, conjunction, interrogative | **qu'**il vienne, **qu'**est-ce que | — |
| **si** | conjunction | **s'**il pleut, **s'**ils viennent | only before *il, ils* |
| **lorsque, puisque, quoique** | conjunctions | **lorsqu'**il, **puisqu'**on, **quoiqu'**elle | traditionally before *il(s), elle(s), on, un(e), en*; now often generalized |
| **jusque** | preposition | **jusqu'**à, **jusqu'**ici, **jusqu'**où | — |
| **presque** | adverb | **presqu'**île | only in this word; otherwise *presque entier* |
| **quelque** | determiner | **quelqu'**un, **quelqu'**une | only in these words; otherwise *quelque autre* |

# Remarks
Rmk: 
1. NO elision before *qui*. 
> l'homme **qui** est venu (never \*qu'est venu, except in very colloquial speech)
2. Elision of *si* only happens before *il(s)*, i.e. no elision before *elle, elles, on*
>Elle viendra **si elle** veut.
>Elle viendra **si on** lui demande.
3. *tu* does not elide in standard French (*tu as*), but colloquially *t'as*, *t'es* are very common.
4. No elision of *la* before the word *une* "front page": *la une du journal*.

# h muet vs h aspiré

The letter *h* is never pronounced in French, but words beginning with *h* fall into two groups:

| | h muet 哑音 h | h aspiré 嘘音 h |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Behaves like | a vowel | a consonant |
| Elision | yes: **l'**homme, **l'**heure, **j'**habite | no: **le** héros, **la** haine, **je** hais |
| [[Liaison 联诵 liaison\|Liaison]] | yes: les‿hommes /le.z‿ɔm/ | no: les \| héros /le.e.ʁo/ |
| Special adjective forms | **cet** hôtel, **bel** homme | **ce** héros, **beau** hibou |
| Origin | mostly Latin | mostly Germanic (Frankish), plus some others |

Common words with h aspiré (dictionaries mark them with an asterisk or apostrophe):
> **la** haine 仇恨, **la** honte 羞耻, **le** haut 上部, **la** hache 斧子, **le** hibou 猫头鹰, **le** hasard 偶然, **le** haricot 豆子, **le** hall 大厅, **le** héros 英雄, **la** Hollande, **le** huit (the number), **la** hauteur 高度

Note the pair:
> **le** héros (h aspiré) vs **l'**héroïne (h muet)

# Other exceptions (no elision before a vowel)

Some vowel-initial words behave as if they began with a consonant:
> **le** oui, **le** onze, **le** onzième, **le** huit, **le** huitième
> **le** yaourt, **le** yacht, **le** yoga (initial *y* = /j/)
> **le** ouistiti, **la** ouate (both *la ouate* and *l'ouate* occur)
> **le** un (the number as a noun: *le un est gagnant*), but *l'un des deux*

Also no elision when a word is cited metalinguistically: *le « a »*, *le mot « aimer »*.

# Contrast with English

English writes contractions with an apostrophe too (*don't, it's*), but these are optional and stylistic. French elision is **obligatory** in standard writing: \**le ami* or \**je ai* are simply wrong.
