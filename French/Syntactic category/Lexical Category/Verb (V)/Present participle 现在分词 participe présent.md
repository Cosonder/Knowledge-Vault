# Introduction

The present participle (*le participe présent*) is a non-finite form ending in **-ant**. Preceded by *en*, it forms the **gérondif** (副动词). 它比英语的 *-ing* 用得少得多：法语的进行意义通常用现在时或 *être en train de* 表达，名词化用不定式。

# Formation

stem = the 1st person plural of the [[Present indicative 直陈式现在时 indicatif présent|present indicative]] - "-ons", + **-ant** (same stem as the [[Imperfect 未完成过去时 imparfait|imperfect]]).

> nous parl**ons** → parl**ant**
> nous finiss**ons** → finiss**ant**
> nous vend**ons** → vend**ant**
> nous fais**ons** → fais**ant**
> nous mange**ons** → mange**ant**, nous commenç**ons** → commenç**ant**

Three exceptions:

| infinitive | present participle |
| ---------- | ------------------ |
| être       | **étant**          |
| avoir      | **ayant**          |
| savoir     | **sachant**        |

Compound form (anteriority): *ayant / étant* + [[Past participle 过去分词 participe passé|past participle]]: *ayant fini, étant parti(e)(s), s'étant levé(e)(s)*.

Pronominal verbs keep the reflexive pronoun agreeing with the subject: *en **me** levant, en **nous** promenant*.

# Use of the present participle

The verbal present participle is **invariable**.

## Replacing a relative clause (*qui* + verb), mainly written

> Je cherche une personne **parlant** chinois.
> = une personne qui parle chinois.

## Cause, circumstance (participial clause)

> **Étant** malade, il est resté chez lui.
> -> Being ill, he stayed at home.

> **Ayant terminé** son travail, elle est sortie.
> -> Having finished her work, she went out.

The implied subject should be the subject of the main clause.

# Gérondif: *en* + present participle

Invariable; its subject is **always the subject of the main verb**.

## Simultaneity

> Il chante **en prenant** sa douche.
> -> He sings while taking a shower.

*tout en* stresses simultaneity or opposition:

> **Tout en** **étant** riche, il vit simplement.
> -> Although rich, he lives simply.

## Manner, means

> Elle est entrée **en courant**.
> -> She ran in. (英语用动词表方式，法语用副动词)

> C'est **en forgeant** qu'on devient forgeron.
> -> Practice makes perfect.

## Condition

> **En partant** maintenant, tu arriveras à l'heure.
> -> If you leave now, you'll arrive on time.

# Present participle vs gérondif

| participe présent                            | gérondif                                  |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| no *en*                                      | *en* + -ant                               |
| qualifies a noun (≈ relative clause) or gives cause | modifies the verb (time, manner, condition) |
| subject can be another noun                  | subject = subject of the main verb        |
| *J'ai vu Paul **sortant** du cinéma.* (Paul was coming out) | *J'ai vu Paul **en sortant** du cinéma.* (I was coming out) |

# Verbal adjective (*adjectif verbal*)

Many present participles are also adjectives; then they **agree**, and some differ in spelling.

> des enfants **obéissants** (adj.) / des enfants **obéissant** à leurs parents (participle)

| participle  | adjective      |
| ----------- | -------------- |
| fatiguant   | fatigant       |
| convainquant | convaincant   |
| différant   | différent      |
| précédant   | précédent      |
| négligeant  | négligent      |

# English equivalent

*-ing* forms: *speaking, having finished*; the gérondif ≈ *while / by / on + -ing*.
