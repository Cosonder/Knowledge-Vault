# Definition

A **lexeme**（词位，lexème）is the abstract unit of the [[Lexicon 词库 lexique|lexicon]]: one word considered independently of its inflected forms. *va, allons, irai, allé* are four **word-forms**（词形，formes fléchies）of a single lexeme, conventionally written in capitals: ALLER. Lexemes are the units a dictionary lists and the basic objects of [[Lexicology 词汇学 lexicologie|lexicology]].

The lexeme is the [[Emic units 位单位 unités émiques|emic unit]] of the vocabulary; the word-forms that actually occur in speech and writing are its etic realizations.

The form chosen to name a lexeme is the **lemma**（词目，lemme / forme canonique）: in French, the infinitive for verbs, the singular for nouns, the masculine singular for adjectives and determiners.

词位是词汇系统中的抽象单位：同一个词的所有屈折形式（变位、性数变化）合起来算一个词位。词典按词位立条，用词目（lemma）作为条目形式。

# Lexeme, word-form, lemma

|                | example                    | level                                 |
| -------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| lexeme 词位    | ALLER                      | abstract unit of the lexicon          |
| word-form 词形 | *va, allons, irai, allé*   | concrete inflected form in a sentence |
| lemma 词目     | *aller*                    | citation form that names the lexeme   |

# Examples (French)

> CHEVAL: *cheval, chevaux*
> BEAU: *beau, bel, belle, beaux, belles*
> ÊTRE: *suis, est, sommes, étais, fus, serai, soit, été*… — suppletive stems, see [[Inflection 屈折变化 flexion|inflection]]

Inflection stays inside one lexeme; derivation creates a new one (see [[Morphology 构词学 morphologie|morphology]]):
> *chante, chantons, chanté* — one lexeme CHANTER
> *chanteur, chanson* — new lexemes derived from it

Homonyms are different lexemes that share a form:
> *le livre* (book) / *la livre* (pound) — two lexemes, two genders
> *suis* is a form of both ÊTRE (*je suis là*) and SUIVRE (*je te suis*)

A lexeme can consist of several orthographic words: *pomme de terre*, *chemin de fer*, *tout à coup* (see [[Phraseology 熟语学 phraséologie|phraseology]]).

# Remarks

1. In the French functionalist tradition (Martinet), *lexème* often means only the lexical morpheme of a word, as opposed to the grammatical morpheme: in *chantons*, *chant-* is the lexème and *-ons* the morphème grammatical. 阅读法语语言学文献时要注意这一术语差异。
2. Counting words depends on the unit: *Le chat voit les chats* has 5 word-forms (*le, chat, voit, les, chats*) but only 3 lexemes (LE, CHAT, VOIR).
3. 汉语几乎没有屈折变化，所以词位与“词”基本重合：“看了”“看过”中的“了、过”是独立的虚词，而不是“看”的屈折形式。
