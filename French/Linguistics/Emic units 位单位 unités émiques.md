Etic units

# Definition

**Emic units**（位单位，unités émiques）are abstract, system-internal units defined by their function or contrast within a given system, as perceived by its users. They are opposed to **etic units**（非位单位/素单位，unités étiques）, the concrete, observable realizations described from outside, independently of any particular system.

The terms were coined by Kenneth Pike (1954) from *phon**emic*** vs *phon**etic***: the [[Phoneme 音位 phonème|phoneme]] is emic, the [[Phone 音素 phone|phone]] is etic. By analogy, many units in linguistics and the humanities are named with the suffix **-eme**（-位，-ème）.

| emic (-eme) | etic realization | variants | field |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| phoneme 音位 | phone 音素 | allophone | [[Phonology 音韵学 phonologie\|phonology]] |
| morpheme 语素 | morph | allomorph | [[Morphology 构词学 morphologie\|morphology]] |
| grapheme 字位 | glyph 字形 | allograph | [[Orthography 正字法 orthographe\|orthography]] |
| lexeme 词位 | word-form 词形 | — | [[Lexicology 词汇学 lexicologie\|lexicology]] |

# Examples
* [[Phoneme 音位 phonème]] — /ʁ/ in French, realized as \[ʁ\] or \[χ\]
* grapheme — the letter ⟨a⟩, whether written *a*, ɑ, A
* morpheme — French plural, realized as *-s*, *-x* (*chevaux*), or zero in speech
* lexeme — ALLER, realized as *va, allons, irai*
* sememe（义位）— a unit of meaning, see [[Semantics 语义学 sémantique|semantics]]
* meme — a unit of cultural transmission (Dawkins)
* mytheme — a minimal unit of myth (Lévi-Strauss)
* narreme — a minimal unit of narrative
* ludeme — a unit of game play (e.g. "roll a die")
* tagmeme — a unit of grammar in Pike's tagmemics

# French illustration: the plural morpheme

> écrit: le**s** chat**s**, de**s** journ**aux** — allomorphs *-s*, *-aux*
> oral: /le ʃa/ — the plural is marked only on the determiner
> before a vowel: le**s‿**amis /lezami/ — [[Liaison 联诵 liaison|liaison]] reveals /z/

One emic unit (PLURAL), several etic realizations depending on medium and context.

# Remarks

1. In anthropology, "emic" means the insider's perspective and "etic" the outside observer's — the same distinction extended to cultures.
2. 中文译名中 “-位” 对应 -eme（音位、词位、义位），而 “素” 有时对应 -eme（语素 morpheme），有时对应 etic 单位（音素 phone），需要注意。
